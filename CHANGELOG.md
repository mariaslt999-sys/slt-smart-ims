# Changelog - SLT Smart IMS

## [2.1.0] - 2026-11-18

- Full 2-Way real-time synchronization between WooCommerce and Google Sheets via Google Apps Script (SLT_Google_Sheets_TwoWay_Sync.gs).

- Team Marketing Hub & Auto-Post Queue webhook integration ([slt_team_marketing_hub] and [slt_ims_portal] shortcodes).
- Role-based frontend portal hiding sensitive cost/profit data from general marketing staff.
