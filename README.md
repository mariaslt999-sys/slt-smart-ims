## Overview
**SLT SMART IMS** is an open-source, all-in-one Inventory Management System (IMS), Sales & Receivables CRM, 2-Way Google Sheets Sync Bridge, WooCommerce Product Synchronizer, and 1-Click Bilingual (English + Arabic) Social Media & Haraj Marketing Hub built by **Shahzad Babar (Smart Life Teck)**.

## Key Features
1. **Team Marketing & 1-Click Copy Hub (`[slt_team_marketing_hub]`):** Instant 1-click copy buttons for English SEO titles, Saudi Arabic Haraj titles, full specifications, price cards, TikTok/Reels captions, WhatsApp customer replies, and 30-second AI voiceover scripts.
2. **Live Stock & Financial Dashboard (`[slt_ims_portal]`):** Real-time tracking of purchased units, completed sales, live stock counts, low-stock alerts, receivables, and net profit.
3. **New Purchase & Quick Sale CRM:** Auto-calculates landed unit costs, deducts live stock upon sale completion, and tracks customer payment follow-ups.
4. **2-Way Google Sheets + WooCommerce Sync:** Synchronizes inventory, prices, and orders between WordPress/WooCommerce and Google Sheets via `SLT_Google_Sheets_TwoWay_Sync.gs` or 1-Click CSV Import.
5. **AI Auto-Post & Make.com Queue:** Schedules and triggers social media posts across YouTube, TikTok, Facebook, Instagram, and Haraj KSA.

## Installation
1. Upload the `slt-smart-ims` folder to `/wp-content/plugins/` or upload `slt-smart-ims-opensource.zip` via **WordPress Admin → Plugins → Add New → Upload Plugin**.
2. Activate **SLT SMART IMS** in WordPress.
3. Open **SLT SMART IMS → Tab 6 (Google Sheets 2-Way Sync & Upload)** to connect your Google Sheet IDs and optional Apps Script / Make.com Webhooks.
